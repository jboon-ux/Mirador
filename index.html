How Mirador works

1

Capture live

The refund button opens the camera, never the gallery. No screenshots, no old photos, no images found online.

2

Check five signals

When it was taken, whether an editing or AI app touched it, whether it was used before, and whether it shows the problem on the right item.

3

Decide and seal

Honest buyers are refunded at once. Fakes are stopped. Every photo gets a fingerprint, so it can't be changed later.

Who it's for

Marketplaces and delivery apps

Add live capture to your refund flow through an API. Refund honest buyers instantly, stop fake photos, and spend fewer agent minutes per dispute.

Sellers and restaurants

Stop paying for damage that never happened, without having to prove anything yourself.
